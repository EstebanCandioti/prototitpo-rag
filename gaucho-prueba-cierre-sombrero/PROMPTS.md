# Prompts y procedencia

Modo: herramienta integrada de generación de imágenes (`image_gen`).
Técnica: poses referenciadas, extracción por componentes, escala compartida y registro; montaje con las poses originales en los extremos. Recursos para la web Entre cartas.

## Gesto con mazo rojo

Use case: identity-preserve.
Asset type: a four-pose horizontal animation strip for an existing illustrated gaucho web character.
Input image 1 is the exact canonical RESPONSE pose and identity: half-lidded, serious, mysterious eyes under the hat. Input image 2 is the exact final REST pose, with the hat hiding the eyes. These are the same character. Preserve this exact ink illustration style, colors, proportions, red V-neck poncho, dark shadow face, brown hat with black band, five red-backed cards, wooden table and small deck on its viewer-right corner, cream trousers, boots, stool. No mouth, nose, eyebrows or neck scarf.
Generate ONLY FOUR NEW full-character poses, separated left-to-right in a SINGLE HORIZONTAL ROW with ample transparent gaps and outer margin. Every pose contains the COMPLETE seated figure, entire hat, table, legs, stool and boots; same size, camera, position and baseline throughout. The tabletop and all artwork below it must stay visually identical, not wobble or move. Actual transparent background, no checkerboard.
The character's hand on VIEWER-LEFT keeps holding the complete fan of EXACTLY FIVE cards in the same place in all poses. Only the hand on VIEWER-RIGHT releases the fan and moves up to the front right edge of the hat brim.
Pose 1: the freed viewer-right hand is rising toward the hat; index and middle finger together extended toward the brim. Keep the exact half-lidded eyes from input 1, not round alert eyes.
Pose 2: those TWO fingertips touch the front-right brim with a small clear salute gesture, elbow naturally bent; retain the half-lidded eyes and dark shadow face.
Pose 3: the same fingertips gently pull the brim downward, fully hiding the eyes as in input 2. Hat stays on the head. Five cards still held by the other hand.
Pose 4: brim remains lowered, eyes hidden, the viewer-right hand lowers and approaches its original position on the fan again, almost matching input 2.
The gesture is discreet and self-possessed, not waving or smiling. Never add cards, props or limbs. Keep hands anatomically plausible. Do not change the face or invent new facial detail. No dialogue bubble, typography, numbers, arrows, frames, labels, guide marks or watermark. Artwork has heavy expressive black outlines and the exact textured flat colors of the supplied reference, not 3D. Transparent negative space must separate the four complete pose groups so they can be extracted as individual sprites.

## Variante de mazo azul

Use case: precise-object-edit.
Input image 1 is the EDIT TARGET: a four-pose strip of the gaucho lowering his hat, with transparent background.
Input image 2 is ONLY a supporting reference for the approved BLUE card backs (blue field with cream ornamental medallion).
Make a blue-deck variant of image 1. Change ONLY the FIVE card backs in the hand and the TOP card back of the small deck on the table, in each of the four poses, from red with cream diamond to the approved blue with cream medallion shown in image 2.
Strictly preserve all four poses, their order, spacing, pixel dimensions, alignment, hand and two-finger gesture, half-lidded eyes in the first two poses, hidden eyes in the last two, red poncho, hat, table, legs, chair, boots, outlines, transparency and texture. The poncho MUST STAY RED. No other color changes. Same exact four complete characters in one horizontal row. Do not add or remove cards. Do not add text, arrows, frames, labels, background or shadows. This is a color/texture edit of the card interiors only, not a new interpretation of the character or scene.
